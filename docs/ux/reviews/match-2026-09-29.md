## Evidence and method

- **Screenshots:** read 34 of the 328 in `docs/ui/screenshots/match/`. That covers every captured state at 390 × 844 in fa or en, plus MA-02 at 360 × 800, 768 × 1024, 1024 × 768, 1440 × 900, and 844 × 390, and MA-13b at 844 × 390.
- **Code read in full:**
  - `features/match/*`

  - the public API of `packages/game3d/src/scene` (`types.ts`, the `GameBoard.tsx` structure)
  - `docs/ui/3d-art-direction.md` §12 and `design-system.md` §9.11 (the UI specialist's deviations)
- **Walked live:** Playwright against the local stack with the suite's mocks.
  - The seven `match behaviour` tests pass.
  - Probes:
    - Space on a focused control
    - text at 200% at 844 × 390, 390 × 844, and 360 × 800

    - the status of every route the match screens link to
- **Not verified:** real-device frame rate (§16), tap and drag on the 3D board (no e2e drives the canvas), and rotation while a sheet is open.

## What works well

- **The board gets the space:**
  - In portrait the canvas holds only board, checkers, dice, and the 3D move markers. The top strip, bars, and action bar are HTML on solid surfaces.
  - Roll, Confirm, Undo, and Double sit in the bottom 12% at 360, 390, and 430 (e2e checks 390).
- **Not color alone:**
  - Checker sides carry a star or rings, repeated as the swatch in each bar.
  - Legal destinations show a ring and dot with the die digit.
  - Used dice are struck through and labeled "used" for screen readers.
  - The Crawford label, the timer warning, and timeouts use an icon plus text.
- **Automatic actions are announced:** forced move, no move, timeout moves, auto-take (stated in MA-07 before it happens), and the timeout count "n of limit" with a persistent last warning.
- **Disconnects:**
  - MA-10 shows the forfeit countdown, the turn line, "Retry now", and a way out.
  - MA-11 shows the opponent's countdown on their bar, and restores it after a re-sync from `match.state.grace` (Q2 resolved).
  - MA-19 explains the refund and offers "Cancel match".
- **Money and consequences:**
  - MA-08 states points, kind, cube, and score after for a game resign. For a match resign it states the entry lost and the opponent's payout. Before the first roll it becomes "Cancel match".
  - MA-13b coin rows use signs and words.
  - An unaffordable "Play again" after a loss shows lower tiers and the bot with **no shop link**.
- **Fair dice and privacy:**
  - MA-03 shows `seed_commit` with copy.
  - MA-13b publishes the seed with copy and "Verify dice", and says "Only you and @x can watch this replay". There is no share or copy-link action anywhere.
- **Keyboard and screen reader:** MA-18 completes a full turn from a list of targets of at least 44 px (e2e), and "Read the board" gives a text summary.
- **API gaps closed:** `match.state` now carries `rules` (Q1) and `grace` (Q2). `MatchSummary` carries `elo_delta`, `xp`, and `coins` for MA-14 (Q6).

## Issues

### Blocking

**M-01 · blocking (needs the user's decision) · MA-02 board input · every phone viewport**
- **What happens:**
  - Checkers measure 33 px at 360 × 800, 35 px at 390 × 844, and 39 px at 430 × 932, and about 22 px at 844 × 390.
  - The hit target of a point is its triangle strip, about 35 px wide in portrait. The 3D targets fall outside the automated check, which scans only HTML.
  - The UI specialist's geometry is sound. The short axis needs about 9.2 checker widths, so at 360 px no full-board layout can reach 44 px.
- **What should happen:** CLAUDE.md §11.1 requires checkers of at least 44 CSS px on a 360 px portrait screen, and §11.7 requires targets of at least 44 × 44 px. An agent can't waive a CLAUDE.md rule.
- **Fix (a decision for the user, raised by the main agent):**
  - **Recommended:** amend §11.1 and §11.7 for the board to: "checkers ≥ 32 CSS px at 360 × 800 portrait; each point's hit area ≥ 44 px along the point and ≥ 32 px across; the bar and trays are whole-region targets; MA-18 offers every move as ≥ 44 px controls."
    - This meets WCAG 2.2 AA 2.5.8 through its "equivalent control" exception.
    - Then add a test that measures the projected hit areas at 360 × 800 and 844 × 390.
  - **Not recommended:** a zoomed half-board. It hides the position, which matters more in backgammon than target size.
  - M-06 recovers most of the landscape shortfall either way.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §11.1 (Scene), §11.7 (targets); match.md §8 Targets, AC 1.

**M-02 · blocking · MA-02 at 200% text · 844 × 390, 360 × 800 (probe)**
- **What happens:**
  - The screen is fixed at `100dvh` with `overflow: hidden`, so the board takes whatever height the bars leave.
  - At 200% text:
    - 844 × 390: the board region is **0 px wide**. The bars and action column take the whole screen.
    - 360 × 800: the board is **63 px tall**. "Confirm move" breaks mid-word into "Confir / m move", and "Reactions" is clipped at the edge.
    - 390 × 844 fa: the board keeps 238 px.
  - The suite checks only horizontal overflow at 200%, so this passes CI.
- **What should happen:** Text scales to 200% with no loss of content or function (§11.7, WCAG 1.4.4). The board is the product and must stay usable.
- **Fix:** In `LiveMatch.tsx`:
  - Give the board row a minimum: portrait `minmax(min(55svh, 100vw), 1fr)`, landscape board column `minmax(55vw, 1fr)`.
  - Let the rest of the screen scroll vertically when the minimum can't be met (the board stays in view; bars and action bar scroll in the page).
  - Bars at 200%: one line for the name with ellipsis. Level, rating, and pips move into MA-15 and MA-03.
  - Action labels: `overflow-wrap: normal; word-break: keep-all; hyphens: none`, and allow 2 lines.
  - In landscape at 200%: the start column scrolls on its own, and the action column keeps only the primary and the reactions icon button (labeled).
- **Test:** extend the 200% check. The board box is at least 240 × 240 at 360 × 800, 390 × 844, and 844 × 390, and every button has `scrollWidth <= clientWidth`.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §11.7 (200% text), §16 Responsive; WCAG 2.2 1.4.4; match.md §8 "Text size 200%", AC 28.

**M-03 · blocking · match logic in `apps/mobile` · code structure**
- **What happens:** Non-visual game rules are written in the app:
  - `features/match/rules.ts`: `lossKind`, a copy of the engine's `loss_kind`; `gamePoints`; and `readClock`, the split between turn time and time bank.
  - `panels.tsx`:
    - `pipsOf`, which duplicates `game-core` `pipCount` (the file already imports `pipCount`)
    - `countAt`
    - the notation's perspective handling
  - `LiveMatch.tsx`: the opponent-to-viewer numbering map (`25 − point`) and the `waitingJoin` predicate.
  - `MatchOverlays.tsx`: the `beforeFirstRoll` predicate.

  Phase 2 would duplicate all of it, and none of it is unit-tested.
- **What should happen:** CLAUDE.md §2 rule 14 says every non-visual rule lives in `packages/*`. The same ruling was made for wallet W-02 and play P-04.
- **Fix:**
  - Move these to `packages/game-core`: `lossKind`, `gamePoints`, `readClock`, `countAt`, `toViewerPoint`, `isBeforeFirstRoll`, and `isWaitingForOpponent`. Reuse `pipCount`.
  - Unit tests:
    - `lossKind` on the engine's reference positions (single, gammon, backgammon with a checker on the bar or in the winner's home)
    - `readClock` at and after `turn_seconds`
    - the viewer mapping for bar and off
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §2 rule 14, §4 (`game-core`), §16.

**M-04 · blocking · MA-13b and MA-14 · all viewports (probe)**
- **What happens:**
  - "View replay" and "Verify dice" (MA-13b), "Watch replay" (MA-14), and "Match history" (MA-14 not found) open `/replay/{id}`, `/replay/{id}?verify=1`, and `/me/matches`. All three return **404**.
  - Verifying the dice is the fairness promise of §6.3, and it ends at a 404.
- **What should happen:** The result flow ends on working screens. The replay viewer is part of the same §17 step 8.
- **Fix:** Either:
  - ship `/replay/[id]` and `/me/matches` from `history-replay.md` in this release, or
  - until they exist, hide the three actions and "Match history", and keep the seed with its copy button plus the sentence "You'll be able to verify every roll from your match history."
- **Test:** every link and navigation in MA-13b and MA-14 returns a status below 400.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §6.3, §17 step 8, §20.2; match.md §2 exits, AC 19 and AC 26.

**M-05 · blocking · MA-02 dice chips · all viewports (contrast)**
- **What happens:** A used die's digit uses `text.disabled` (`#857a6e` in the dark scheme) on the action bar's `surface` (`#1c1714`). That is about **4.2:1** for 14 px label text. The chips are information, not disabled controls, so the WCAG exemption for inactive components doesn't apply.
- **What should happen:** Text is at least 4.5:1 (WCAG 2.2 AA 1.4.3; match.md §8 Contrast).
- **Fix:** In `parts.tsx` `DiceChips`, draw used dice in `textSecondary` (check it is at least 4.5:1 on `surface` in both schemes), keeping the strike-through and the hidden "used" text. Add the pair to the token contrast test.
- **Ref:** WCAG 1.4.3; match.md §8.

### Major

**M-06 · major · MA-02 landscape phones · 844 × 390, 932 × 430**
- **What happens:** The start column (12–16rem) and the action column (9–11rem) leave the board about 46% of the width (`ma02-moving--fa-844x390`). Checkers are about 22 px, the smallest anywhere, although the board could use the full 390 px height.
- **What should happen:** In landscape, the board gets the space (§11.0 mobile, P§13).
- **Fix:**
  - Set the start column to `minmax(10rem, 12rem)` and the action column to `minmax(7.5rem, 8.5rem)`.
  - Dice chips go in one row above the primary.
  - The board is height-limited, not width-limited.
- **Test:** at 844 × 390 the canvas is at least 55% of the viewport width, and checkers are at least 28 px.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §11.1, §11.7; match.md §6 landscape row.

**M-07 · major · keyboard shortcuts · all viewports with a keyboard (probe)**
- **What happens:** The window `keydown` handler acts whenever no dialog is open, whatever has focus. With focus on the "Match menu" button, Space sent `turn.roll` and the menu did not open (probe). The same applies to Space on "Leave" or on a bar's avatar, and to R, M, D, and Esc while focus is on a switch or a link.
- **What should happen:** Shortcuts never override the focused control (WCAG 2.1.1 and 2.1.4; match.md §6 keyboard). An unintended roll can't be undone.
- **Fix:** In `LiveMatch.tsx`, run shortcuts only when `e.target` is `body`, the board region, or the action bar container, and never for `button`, `a`, `input`, or `[role=button|radio|tab|switch|menuitem]`. List the shortcuts in MA-03 (already done).
- **Test:** focus "Match menu", press Space: the menu opens and no `turn.roll` is sent.
- **Ref:** WCAG 2.1.1, 2.1.4; match.md §6, §8.

**M-08 · major · MA-07 double offered · all viewports**
- **What happens:** "Take (×2)" is a filled primary button and "Drop (lose 1)" is outlined (`ma07-double-offered--fa-390x844`). The UI steers the decision.
- **What should happen:** Both buttons have equal size **and weight** (match.md §3.8 step 5, AC 11).
- **Fix:** In `MatchOverlays.tsx` `DoubleDialog`, give both `variant="outlined"` (or both the same neutral contained style). Keep the text labels.
- **Test:** the two buttons have identical computed background and border.
- **Ref:** match.md AC 11; P§3.

**M-09 · major · MA-11 opponent disconnected · all viewports (probe)**
- **What happens:**
  - The live region announces the disconnect once ("…They have 00:32 to return.").
  - Nothing is announced when it crosses 30 s or 10 s. The probe waited past 30 s and the region text was unchanged.
  - Visually the bar repeats itself: "Disconnected · 00:29 until they forfeit · 29 seconds until @ali_tbz forfeits".
- **What should happen:** Warnings at 30 s and 10 s with text, icon, and announcement (match.md §3.11 step 2, AC 14).
- **Fix:**
  - Announce `match.opponent.warning` once at 30 s and once at 10 s.
  - Keep one visual line, `match.opponent.disconnected` with its time, and switch it to the warning style under 30 s. Drop the appended sentence.
  - Add the haptic and announcement pair at 30 s and 10 s to MA-10 as well (§3.10 step 3).
- **Ref:** match.md §3.10 step 3, §3.11 step 2, §8 live regions; AC 13 and AC 14.

**M-10 · major · MA-01 loader Cancel · first entry to a new match (code reading)**
- **What happens:**
  - For a fresh match the socket attaches only once the scene is ready, so `view` is null during the download.
  - "Cancel" calls `setSheet("leave")`, but `MatchOverlays` opens sheets only when `view !== null`. The tap does nothing during the slow download.
  - The leave sheet then pops up unasked once the state arrives.
- **What should happen:** Every wait has a way out that works (§21.2; match.md MA-01).
- **Fix:** When the match is fresh and there is no `view`, Cancel attaches at once (`attach()`), so the opponent isn't left waiting on a player who wants out. It opens MA-09, whose wording doesn't need the state (entry and opponent come from `match.found`). If attaching fails, Cancel returns to `/play`, where PL-08 shows the match.
- **Test:** block the scene chunk, open a fresh match, press Cancel: MA-09 is visible within 500 ms.
- **Ref:** match.md MA-01, §3.1 step 4; CLAUDE.md §21.2.

**M-11 · major · MA-17 device not supported, active match · all viewports**
- **What happens:**
  - The "Resign match" confirmation says only "Your rating changes as for a loss." It leaves out the coin consequence.
  - When `match.ended` arrives, the screen goes to `/play` without showing the result.
- **What should happen:** The MA-08 match wording: "@x wins the match. You lose your entry of {entry} coins; @x receives {payout}." Afterwards, the MA-14 summary.
- **Fix:** In `MatchScreen.tsx` `Unsupported`:
  - Build the body from `GET matches/{id}` (`entry`). Take the payout from `rules.payout` after attaching, or from the tier.
  - On `match.ended`, reload the summary and render MA-14.
- **Ref:** match.md MA-17, §3.13; P§2 (consequences before a loss of coins).

**M-12 · major · MA-13b coin rows · all viewports, fa especially**
- **What happens:** On a **won** match, the entry and fee rows read "Entry −100 lost" and "Platform fee −20 lost". In fa they read «ورودی −۱۰۰ باخت» and «کارمزد سکو −۲۰ باخت», next to "You receive +180 won" (`ma13b-won--fa-390x844`). The fa word «باخت» means defeat, so a winner is told they "lost" twice.
- **What should happen:** Signs plus plain words, true for the row (match.md §3.15 table; P§9.2).
- **Fix:**
  - New key `match.result.debit`: `−{amount}` / «−{amount}», with no word. Use it for the Entry and Platform fee rows.
  - Keep `signWon` and `signLost` for "You receive" and "Net" only.
  - Pot stays unsigned.
- **Ref:** match.md §3.15 step 2; P§9.2; Nielsen 2.

**M-13 · major · evidence and visual regression**
- **What happens:**
  - The match suite doesn't compare its screenshots (§9.11: "its clocks tick"), so nothing prevents a regression.
  - Landscape en exists only for `ma02-your-roll`, `ma02-moving`, and `ma13b-won`.
  - There are no screenshots for:
    - MA-01, MA-04, MA-15, MA-16
    - the opening roll and tie captions, and the forced and no-move captions
    - "Waiting for @x to take or drop"
    - MA-13b lost (affordable), bot result, and aborted with no entry
  - In `ma11-…` and `ma19-…` the own bar shows the opponent's level and rating (fixture).
- **What should happen:** Every screen and state as visual regression at the §11.7 matrix (§16).
- **Fix:**
  - `match.spec.ts`: `page.clock.install({ time })` before navigation and a fixed `server_now` in `clockFixture`. Then call `screenshot()` (`toHaveScreenshot`) like the other suites.
  - Add the missing cases and 932 × 430 en for all WIDE cases.
  - Fix `playerFixture` for side 0.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §16 Responsive; match.md AC 28.

### Minor

**M-14 · minor · cube chip position.** The centered cube chip uses `insetInlineStart`, so it switches sides between fa and en. It sits on the board's corner over the tray edge (`ma02-crawford--en-390x844`). Fix: anchor it to the board's side edge, vertically centered, with physical `left` (the board never mirrors, §11.1, P§11). It must not intersect the tray rectangles. Ref: match.md §3.2 Board, §7.

**M-15 · minor · MA-06 bubble.** The opponent's bubble hangs from the bottom of their bar into the board and overlaps the top frame and the first row of checkers (`ma06-bubble--en-390x844`). Fix: place the bubble inside the bar's end area, overlaying the clock for 3 s, or clamp it so its box doesn't intersect the board's playing rect. Test: the bubble rect ∩ the canvas playing area = ∅. Ref: match.md §3.9 step 4, AC 12.

**M-16 · minor · "Show opponent's reactions".** The switch is component state. It resets for every match and every reload. Fix: store it device-locally (`lib/prefs`, localStorage). Ref: match.md §3.9 step 5.

**M-17 · minor · MA-10.**
- There is no haptic at 30 s and 10 s.
- "Leave match screen" doesn't say the countdown continues.

Fix: add the vibration pattern and the line `match.reconnect.leaveNote` («با خروج از صفحه، شمارش معکوس متوقف نمی‌شود.» / "Leaving doesn't stop the countdown."). Ref: match.md §3.10 steps 2 and 3.

**M-18 · minor · MA-19.**
- While waiting for the opponent to join, the action bar says "Opening roll", which suggests the game is about to start.
- "Cancel match" sits at about 56% of the height, above the thumb zone.

Fix: leave the center slot empty in this state, as in the MA-02 table, and place the card's Cancel at the card's bottom, with the card anchored to the lower half of the board. Ref: match.md §4 MA-02 table, §3.12.

**M-19 · minor · unusable dice.** When a die can't be used, the chip shows only a warning icon plus hidden text. Fix: show the caption `match.dice.unusable` («۵ قابل استفاده نیست» / "The 5 can't be used") above the action bar. Ref: match.md §3.5 step 7.

**M-20 · minor · MA-13b details.**
- The seed is truncated at the end, not in the middle.
- The suspended state's disabled "Play again" has no "Details".

Fix: truncate the seed in the middle (`first 8 … last 8`, with the full value in the copy button and the accessible name), and add `account.suspended.details` → `/account/status`. Ref: match.md §3.15, §5 Suspended.

**M-21 · minor · 1024 × 768.** Move history is reachable only through the menu, and the start panel has no toggle. Fix: add a "Moves" tab to the end panel (Moves · Reactions · Match info, as in the md landscape row). Ref: match.md §6 (the condition of ruling 4).

**M-22 · minor · MA-08 copy.** "Score after: 1 – 3 This ends the match: @ali_tbz wins." has no sentence break (`ma08-resign--en-390x844`). Fix: render the three sentences as separate lines. Ref: Nielsen 8.

**M-23 · minor · own avatar label.** The own bar's avatar button is named "View profile: tester1" but opens the match menu. Fix: use `match.menu.title` as its name when `self`. Ref: WCAG 2.5.3 (label in name), 4.1.2.

**M-24 · minor (owner: main agent) · move history after a re-sync.** Every full `match.state` (reconnect, reload) empties MA-04, and MA-04 says so. Fix: add `history` (or `GET matches/{id}/moves` for players) with the turn list, so MA-04 is complete. Ref: match.md MA-04.

## Heuristics and thumb reach

- **Nielsen:**
  - Strong on 1 (status: turn, clocks, time bank, automatic actions), 5 (MA-08 and MA-07 state consequences first), and 9 (mapped error toasts reset the board).
  - Gaps:
    - 3, user control: M-07, M-10
    - 2, match with the real world: M-12
    - 4, consistency: M-08
    - 8, minimalist design: M-09 duplicate text, M-22
- **Thumb reach (390 × 844 portrait):**
  - Roll, Confirm, Undo, Double, and Reactions sit at 88–98% of the height.
  - The MA-07 buttons are at about 69%. The MA-08, MA-09, and MA-13b footers are in the bottom 20%.
  - Only MA-19 "Cancel match" is outside the thumb zone, at about 56% (M-18).

## Verdict

**Changes required.** Five blocking issues (M-01 to M-05) and eight major issues (M-06 to M-13). M-01 needs the user's decision on CLAUDE.md §11.1 before this step can be signed off.
