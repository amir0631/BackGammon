## New issues

### Blocking

**W-21 · blocking · WA-01 wallet home · 768 × 1024 portrait (any wallet container of about 36–42rem), fa and en**
- **What happens:**
  - The W-05 change starts two columns at a 36rem container, with the summary column at `minmax(17.5rem, 22.5rem)`. At 768 wide with the side rail, the container is about 616 px, which leaves the history column about 230 px.
  - The ledger row then switches to its narrow mode (`@container (max-width: 16rem)`), where `.tx-main` collapses to one character per line.
  - "Received from @ali_tbz" and "Withdrawal request" are spelled vertically, letter by letter, over the whole screen height (`wa01-wallet--fa-768x1024`, `wa01-wallet--en-768x1024`).
  - The history is unreadable. At 844 × 390 and 1024 × 768 the layout is fine.
- **What should happen:** md shows summary and history side by side with readable rows. Otherwise it stays in one column.
- **Fix:**
  - `WalletHomeScreen.tsx`:
    - two columns from a 36rem container with `minmax(16rem, 1fr) minmax(18rem, 1.25fr)`, so the history column is never under 18rem; or
    - raise `WIDE` to the width at which the history gets at least 18rem.
  - `Ledger.tsx` narrow mode: keep icon, text, and chevron on the first line, and put the amount on its own line. `.tx-main` gets `flex: 1 1 0; min-width: 8rem`.
  - Regenerate the WA-01 and WA-02 baselines.
- **Test:** at 600 × 960, 768 × 1024, and 1024 × 768 in fa and en, every `.tx-main` is at least 128 px wide, and no text node is taller than 3 lines.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §11.7 md row; wallet.md §6, AC 37; previous W-05.

**W-22 · blocking · `/wallet/withdraw` first-load JS · all viewports**
- **What happens:** `design-system.md` reports 252 kB gzipped for `/wallet/withdraw` after step 6, over the 250 kB budget for non-game routes. The growth comes from the app-wide socket provider and the resume-banner plumbing added by the play build. `/wallet/transfer` is at 247 kB, 3 kB from the limit.
- **What should happen:** Non-game routes stay under 250 kB (CLAUDE.md §11.4). The user on unstable 4G (P1) pays for every kB.
- **Fix:**
  - `lib/socket.tsx`: load `GameSocket` with `import()` on the first `connect()`. Keep only the context and a lightweight status in the root layout.
  - `ResumeMatchBanner`: keep it free of socket code (it only needs `GET me/matches/active`).
  - Alternatively, lazy-load the WD-05 code step and `IbanField`.
  - Add a CI size check that fails above 250 kB for every non-game route.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §11.4 (JS < 250 KB gzipped); design-system.md bundle note.

### Minor

**W-23 · minor · WA-04 Get coins · all viewports**
- **What happens:** When `support_contact` isn't available, the sheet renders "Contact support: our support team" («…: پشتیبانی»). Its copy button copies that fallback phrase.
- **Cause and effect:** The wallet mocks have no `support_contact`, so every WA-04 baseline shows the fallback state (`wa04-get-coins--fa-390x844`), not the real one.
- **Fix:**
  - `SupportTopup.tsx`: with no configured channel, show one sentence (`support.contact.neutral`, "Contact support from the Account tab.") with no channel line and no copy button.
  - Add `support_contact` to `wallet-mocks.ts` configs and regenerate the WA-04 baselines.
- **Ref:** previous W-04; wallet.md §3.3 step 2.3.

**W-24 · minor · TR-02 and WD-03 amount steps · all viewports**
- **What happens:** For a limit error, the same three-line message appears twice: under the field and again under the disabled "Continue" (`tr02-amount-limit--en-390x844`).
- **Fix:** When the field error is visible, set the disabled reason to the short key `transfer.amount.disabledFix` / `withdraw.amount.disabledFix` («مقدار بالا را اصلاح کنید» / "Fix the amount above"). Keep the full rule text only under the field. `aria-describedby` still points to the field error.
- **Ref:** Nielsen 8; P§2.2.
