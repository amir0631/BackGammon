## What works well

- **Money:**
  - PL-03 shows every row from server values: entry, toman equivalent, "Platform fee (10% of the pot of 200)", winner receives, balance, and balance "after the match starts".
  - The primary button carries the entry, and a double tap sends one `queue.join`.
  - The timing note explains when the entry is taken and that cancelling is free.
- **Nothing is pre-selected:**
  - PL-02 checks only the tapped tier.
  - PL-04 has no level, variant, or length chosen.
  - Continue and Start show their reason in words.
- **Ways out:**
  - PL-06 Cancel works from the button, Back, and Esc, with no confirmation, in the bottom 12% of the screen.
  - The long-wait line is neutral and offers "Change table" and "Play vs bot".
  - The offline variant says plainly "You're not in the queue while offline", pauses the timer, and keeps Cancel.
- **Race handling:** PL-07 "race" never drops a found match. It states that the entry was taken and offers "Cancel this match" (MA-19).
- **Insufficient coins:** PL-05 lists lower tiers, then the bot, then "Get coins" as a plain text action. MA-13b uses it without the shop after a loss.
- **Bots:** the Bot label (icon plus text) appears on the practice card, in the PL-04 title, and in every level row. Bot names come from `play.bot.name`, never the raw username.
- **Blocked states:**
  - Unsupported, suspended, in-match, and offline each disable play with a visible reason that is linked by `aria-describedby`.
  - PL-08 shows opponent, score, and whose turn it is, in words.
- **API gaps closed by the main agent:**
  - `Tier` now has `rake_pct`, `pot`, and `payout` (Q1).
  - `GET me/matches/active` returns opponent, score, and turn (Q7).
  - The api-client re-sends `queue.join` after a reconnect (Q3).
- **RTL:** layout, chips, and the back arrow mirror. The elapsed timer is LTR-isolated in Persian digits, and usernames are isolated.

## Issues

### Blocking

**P-01 · blocking · PL-03 join confirmation · all viewports (found by a live probe)**
- **What happens:**
  - The cost block uses `balance ?? 0` (`PlaySheet.tsx`, `known`). When `GET wallet` hasn't loaded yet, or has failed, PL-03 shows "Your balance **0 coins**" and "Balance after the match starts **-100 coins**" (with an ASCII hyphen).
  - "Pay 100 coins and search" stays enabled. The affordability check `balance !== null && balance < entry` is skipped, so `queue.join` is sent.
  - This is reachable by opening `/play?again=…` from MA-13b before the wallet has loaded.
- **What should happen:** Every coin spend shows the real current balance and the balance after before the user confirms. A made-up 0 breaks the CLAUDE.md non-negotiable and P§2.1.
- **Fix:**
  - In `PlaySheet.tsx`, never default the balance.
  - While `wallet.summary` is null, render the balance rows as a skeleton.
  - Disable the primary with the new key `play.join.balanceUnknown` («در حال دریافت موجودی…» / "Getting your balance…").
  - If the wallet read failed, add a Retry that calls `wallet.refresh()`.
- **Test:** with `GET /wallet` answering 500, PL-03 contains no "0 coins" and no "-" amount, the primary is disabled, and no `queue.join` is sent.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md UX non-negotiable (cost, balance, balance after); P§2.1; play.md §3.3, AC 2.

**P-02 · blocking · PL-03 · 844 × 390 and 932 × 430 landscape; 360 × 800 portrait**
- **What happens:**
  - In landscape, the dialog's sticky footer (Pay and Cancel, about 150 px) leaves room for the title, the summary, and the Cost row only. The fee, winner receives, balance, and balance-after rows sit under the footer while "Pay 100 coins and search" is on screen (probe: "Balance after" at y = 378–398 in a 390 px viewport, Pay at y = 250).
  - The committed `pl03-confirm--fa-844x390` only looks complete because it was captured after scrolling.
  - At 360 × 800 the timing note ("Your entry is taken when an opponent is found…") is cut off under the footer (`pl03-confirm--en-360x800`).
  - This is the same defect as wallet W-01.
- **What should happen:** The user can't reach the primary without having seen the whole cost block and the timing note.
- **Fix:**
  - Give PL-03 the inline footer that TR-03 and WD-04 use (`TaskFlow` `footerMode="inline"`). Expose it on `useCostConfirmationParts` (`footerMode`) and in `BottomSheet`, so the footer renders after the timing note.
  - Keep sticky footers for PL-02, PL-04, and PL-05.
- **Test:** at 844 × 390, 360 × 800, and 390 × 844, no fixed or sticky element intersects the `dl` or the timing note, and the primary's top is below the timing note's bottom.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md UX non-negotiable; P§2.1; play.md §3.3 and §6 (landscape); wallet review W-01; AC 2 and AC 16.

**P-03 · blocking · PL-04 bot setup and MA-13b bot "Play again" · all viewports**
- **What happens:**
  - Since commit `5adf7aa`, `POST matches/bot` charges `bot.entry_coins` when the admin setting `bot.entry_enabled` is on (`BotMatchView`: `escrow_match_entries`).
  - The UI can't know this: `GET config` doesn't expose it. PL-04 always says "Practice match: free, no rating change, no XP", PL-05 offers "Practice vs bot (free)", and MA-13b's bot "Play again" starts a new match at once.
  - One toggle in the admin panel would charge users with no cost shown and no confirmation.
- **What should happen:** Any coin spend shows the cost, the balance, and the balance after before confirming, with nothing pre-selected (§9, play.md §3.6 step 7).
- **Fix:**
  - **Main agent:** add `bot_entry: {enabled, entry, prize}` to `PublicConfig`, from the snapshot the endpoint uses.
  - **UI, when enabled:**
    - PL-04 shows the P§2 cost block ("Entry", "Prize if you win", balance, balance after).
    - The primary becomes "Pay {entry} coins and start".
    - "free" is removed from `play.practice.desc`, `play.bot.info`, and `play.insufficient.bot`.
    - MA-13b's bot "Play again" opens PL-04 at its cost step instead of starting.
  - **Until the UI ships:** the main agent makes `bot.entry_enabled` read-only (registry validation) so it can't be switched on.
- **Test:** with `bot_entry.enabled`, PL-04 shows the cost rows and MA-13b "Play again" sends no `POST matches/bot` without a confirmation.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md UX non-negotiable; §9; P§2; play.md §3.6 step 7, Q6.

**P-04 · blocking · matchmaking state · code structure**
- **What happens:** `features/play/usePlayQueue.ts` interprets the WebSocket protocol inside `apps/mobile`:
  - the `queue.status` state machine (`waiting`, `left`, and `removed`, with the leaving flag)
  - `match.found` routing (this tab, the found-after-cancel race with its 30 s window, or another tab)
  - the join timeout and the "searching again" logic

  Phase 2 would have to rewrite all of this in `apps/desktop`.
- **What should happen:** CLAUDE.md §2 rule 14 says no protocol handling in `apps/*`. The same ruling was made for wallet W-02.
- **Fix:**
  - Move the state machine to `packages/api-client` (e.g. `queue/QueueFlow.ts`): framework-free, driven by `GameSocket` envelopes, and emitting `QueueState`.
  - Keep a thin `usePlayQueue` hook in the app.
  - Add unit tests for:
    - waiting → offline → waiting (again)
    - `left` that this tab didn't ask for
    - `removed` with reason `balance`
    - `match.found` inside and after the race window
    - found with no search open
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §2 rule 14, §4; wallet review W-02.

### Major

**P-05 · major · PL-01 lobby · 768 × 1024 and 844 × 390 (md and landscape), fa and en**
- **What happens:** The two-column layout gives the side column (practice and rank, about 320 px) more width than each tier card (about 130 px, 2-up). In `pl01-lobby--fa-768x1024`, tier names break inside the phrase («میز ۱۰۰ / سکه‌ای»), "Winner receives" wraps over two lines, and the primary content looks cramped next to secondary cards.
- **What should happen:** Wider screens get more panels, not squeezed primary content. The tier cards (the primary action, J1 step 6) are at least as readable as on a phone.
- **Fix:** In `PlayLobbyScreen.tsx`:
  - At `md`, set the side column to `minmax(15rem, 17.5rem)`.
  - Make `TierGrid` 2-up only when each card gets at least 13rem (`repeat(auto-fill, minmax(13rem, 1fr))` under a named container instead of `mqRail`). Otherwise show one column.
- **Test:** at 768 × 1024 and 844 × 390 in fa and en, each tier name renders on one line at 100% text.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §11.7 (md row, "not a stretched phone screen"); play.md §6.

**P-06 · major · PL-05 after a loss · all viewports**
- **What happens:**
  - The `afterLoss` variant exists only inline in MA-13b. `/play?again=…` doesn't carry where it came from.
  - If MA-13b enables "Play again" because the balance was unknown or stale (`unaffordable` is false while `wallet.summary` is null), the lobby opens PL-03. The server or the client check then opens PL-05 in its **normal** variant, with "Get coins", straight after a loss.
- **What should happen:** Never nudge the user toward buying coins right after a loss (P§9, play.md §3.7 step 3).
- **Fix:**
  - MA-13b adds `&from=loss` when the viewer lost.
  - `PlayLobbyScreen` passes `mode: "afterLoss"` into every PL-05 it opens during that session. `InsufficientOptions` then gets no `onGetCoins`.
  - MA-13b computes `unaffordable` only from a loaded balance. While it is unknown, "Play again" waits with `common.loading`.
- **Test:** lose a match with `GET /wallet` failing, tap "Play again", and have `queue.join` return `WALLET_INSUFFICIENT`. PL-05 has no "Get coins".
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md ethics non-negotiable; P§9.1; play.md §3.7 step 3, AC 11; match.md §3.15 step 4.

**P-07 · major · PL-01 rank card · all viewports (probe)**
- **What happens:** "Leaderboard" links to `/leaderboard`, which returns 404.
- **What should happen:** No link leads to a 404.
- **Fix:** Hide the link, the lg "Top players" link, and the rank card's link role until `/leaderboard` ships (leaderboard.md). The `TopPlayers` snippet can stay.
- **Test:** every `a[href]` on `/play` returns a status below 400.
- **Ref:** Nielsen 5 (error prevention); play.md §2 exits; wallet deviation 1 (don't link to missing routes).

### Minor

**P-08 · minor · screenshot evidence**
- **What happens:**
  - There is no en landscape shot for any play screen: only 844 × 390 fa exists, and 932 × 430 en is missing.
  - All 189 play baselines are duplicated at the root of `docs/ui/screenshots/` (`play-pl01-…png`, byte-identical to `play/pl01-…png`).
  - The `pl05-insufficient` fixtures show the app-bar balance as 1,250 while the sheet says 60.
- **Fix:**
  - Add a 932 × 430 en variant for WIDE cases.
  - Delete the root-level copies.
  - Make the wallet mock's balance match the `WALLET_INSUFFICIENT` balance.
- **Ref:** CLAUDE.md §11.7 last rule, §16 Responsive.

**P-09 · minor · PL-01 suspended · all viewports**
- **What happens:** The screen shows two banners and two "Details" buttons: the shell's AU-13 banner and the lobby's `account.suspended.actionBlocked` banner (`pl01-lobby-suspended--fa-390x844`).
- **Fix:** When the shell's suspension banner is visible, render the lobby reason as an inline line without its own "Details". Keep it as the `aria-describedby` target.
- **Ref:** Nielsen 8; wallet W-17.

**P-10 · minor · PL-06 offline detection (code reading)**
- **What happens:** `usePlayQueue` switches to the offline variant only on socket status `reconnecting`. With `navigator.onLine === false` and a socket that hasn't noticed yet, PL-06 keeps saying "Searching".
- **Fix:** Also switch when `useOnline()` is false.
- **Ref:** play.md §3.4 step 4.

**P-11 · minor · PL-01 · 1440 × 900**
- **What happens:** The tier grid is 3-up with the fourth card alone on a second row (`pl01-lobby--fa-1440x900`).
- **Fix:** Use `minmax(13rem, 1fr)` so 4 cards fit in one row in the 1280 shell.
- **Ref:** play.md §6 lg ("up to 4-up").

## Heuristics and thumb reach

- **Nielsen:**
  - Strong on 1 (visibility of status: elapsed time, offline variant, "Searching again"), 3 (user control: Cancel everywhere, no confirmation to leave the queue), and 9 (errors are specific, with next steps).
  - Gaps:
    - 5, error prevention: P-01, P-06
    - 8, minimalist design: P-09
    - 4, consistency: P-02 repeats a pattern already fixed in wallet
- **Thumb reach (390 × 844 portrait):**
  - The PL-06 Cancel, the PL-07 "Go to match", and every sheet primary sit in the bottom 12%.
  - Tier "Play" pills are at 20–60% of the height, which is acceptable for a scrolling list whose whole card is the target.

## Verdict

**Changes required.** Four blocking issues (P-01 to P-04) and three major issues (P-05 to P-07).
